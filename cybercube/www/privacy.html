Cyber Cube — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Cyber Cube ("the game") is an action puzzle game. This page explains what data the game uses and why.

What we collect

  • Game progress: stars and best clear times for each stage.
  • Leaderboard records: your best clear time for each stage and the nickname you choose. A random nickname (such as PLAYER1234) is used until you change it.
  • An anonymous player ID: a random ID created automatically when you first open the game. It is not linked to your name, email, phone number or any account.

Please do not use your real name or other personal information as your nickname. We do not collect your email address, contacts, location, photos or any other personal information. The game has no ads and no in-app purchases.

How we use it

Your game progress is saved on your device and backed up to our cloud storage so that you do not lose it. It is used only to run the game.

Leaderboard records (nickname and clear time) are public: other players can see them in the game's ranking screen.

Where it is stored

Progress is stored on your device and in Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), operated by Google LLC. See Firebase Privacy and Security.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your data with anyone. Apart from the public leaderboard, your data is not shown to other players.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game or clearing its data removes the progress on your device and disconnects the anonymous ID. Leaderboard records stay until deleted. To ask us to delete the cloud copy or your leaderboard records, contact us at the address below.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
